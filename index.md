# Annie McGrew

![Annie McGrew](profile.jpg)

**PhD Student in Economics**  
University of Massachusetts Amherst

I am a feminist and labor economist studying how labor-market institutions
and capitalist political economy shape reproductive decisions, economic
agency, and inequality.

My research interests include labor economics, feminist economics,
political economy, reproductive justice, reproductive technologies, and
labor-market power.
